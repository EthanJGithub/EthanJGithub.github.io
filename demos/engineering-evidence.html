FraudPulse: evaluate the alerts, not just accuracy

A FastAPI scoring service and React dashboard combine XGBoost fraud scores, IsolationForest anomaly signals and dataset-onboarding checks.

Measured: 0.8755 test PR-AUC on ULB credit-card data. At the validation-selected flag threshold, the test set contains 84 detected frauds, 14 missed frauds and 18 false alerts: 82.35% precision and 85.71% recall across 56,962 transactions.

This is a stratified within-dataset evaluation, not a temporal production benchmark. Anomaly signals are separate from the reported supervised model score.
