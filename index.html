My work pairs modeling depth with real engineering: multi-agent LangGraph pipelines with guardrails and audit trails, real-time YOLO/SAM detection and tracking, fine-tuning and rigorous evaluation, and full-stack delivery on AWS with FastAPI and React. I care about systems that are reliable, explainable, and auditable — built to be trusted.
